03 /About

Research interests & affiliation

My research centers on the intersection of visual perception, privacy, and representation learning.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Electrical Engineering at Korea University, working in the Media Communications Lab. My work explores the separation between what humans can recognize and what machine vision systems can utilize, spanning spatial transformations, frequency-domain manipulation, and generative approaches.
